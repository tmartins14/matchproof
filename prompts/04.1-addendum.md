## 1. Revert the xg_per_shot widening; split season bands from single-match bands

The upper bound was widened from 0.15 to 0.17 to absorb `end_to_end`. That is the wrong fix, for three reasons:

- **It doesn't work.** `make calibrate` on 8 seeds still fails: `end_to_end` seed 7 away is 0.210, above the 0.1835 limit. CI only runs seeds 1–4, so the failure was hidden.
- **It misstates the source.** The cited range describes season averages per team, not single matches.
- **The breach is realistic.** All four breaches are the away side in `end_to_end`, where 3 planted shots (counter goal, open-play goal, penalty at 0.78) carry 1.1–1.2 xG on 12–15 shots. A real 3–3 with a penalty often has xG per shot above 0.17. Organic shots alone average 0.099 and never exceed 0.152.

**Fix:**
- Restore `band: [0.08, 0.15]` for xg_per_shot. Bands apply to the mean across runs, as season figures do.
- Add an optional `match_band` per metric for the single-run check. Single matches vary more than season averages. Set `match_band: [0.04, 0.25]` for xg_per_shot, with `status: provisional` and a note giving the reason above.
- Metrics without a `match_band` keep the existing rule: no run more than 15% of the band width outside the band.
- Document both in the YAML header and `docs/compliance/DATA.md`.

## 2. The out-of-possession block is a rigid template; make it respond to game state

Measured on this branch across 64 team-runs, out of possession: team length 26.9–27.05 m, width exactly 37.8 m, hull area 933–938 m². On `red_card_flip` seed 3:

| Ashford, out of possession | 15 min before the red card | 30 min after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Back-line height | 36.0 m | 32.9 m |
| Team length | 26.9 m | 26.9 m |
| Area | 934 m² | 934 m² |

So the 10-man team barely drops and doesn't compress at all. The demo's positional claims ("back line dropped 8 m since the red card", "outfield area 540 m², from 820 m²") cannot happen.

**Fix in `shape.py`.** The block's depth, length and width respond to these inputs, each a named constant:

- **Down a man:** effective `line_height` drops by about 0.25 and the block compresses, both in length and in width.
- **Leading after 75′:** effective `line_height` drops by about 0.15.
- **Trailing after 70′:** effective `line_height` rises by about 0.2.
- **Higher `press`:** a shorter block along the pitch.

Calibration means must still pass after this.

**Tests:**
- On `red_card_flip` seeds 1–4, the 10-man team's median out-of-possession back line in the 30 minutes after the card is at least 5 m lower than in the 15 minutes before.
- Its hull area is at least 15% smaller over the same comparison.
- Across all calibration runs, team length and area out of possession have a standard deviation above zero (report the values).
