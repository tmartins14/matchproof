## Part 2 — Retune

Before tuning, on main: passes are about 517 per team (18% above 437), mean pass length is 16.6 m (below 19.5) and yellow cards are 1.2 per team (below 1.88).

1. **Event constants:** bring passes, pass length and yellow cards to their reference means within ±12%. Keep the other event metrics passing.
2. **Shape (`shape.py`):**
   - **In possession:** apply the line-height push forward and widen (`WIDTH_IN`), so the back line sits higher and the team stretches.
   - **Out of possession:** pull the whole outfield block toward a mid-block depth set by `line_height`, and compress it along the pitch to about 25–30 m (`COMPACT_OUT`) and across it to about 40 m.
   - The ball pull still moves the block, but must not drag the back line below about 10 m from its own goal unless the ball is inside the defending team's box.
   - Keep each effect a named constant with a one-line comment.
3. Keep prompt 04's dial tests passing. Line height 0.85 must still put the back line at least 8 m higher than 0.2.
