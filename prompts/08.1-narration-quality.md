# Build prompt 08.1 — Narration that reads like commentary

**Mode:** code. **Budget:** about 2 hours. **Branch:** `fix/narration-quality`. **Depends on:** PR #16 merged.

## Context

Read `docs/VOICE.md`, `voice/examples.yaml`, `packages/agents/src/agents/prompts/narrator.v1.md`, `packages/agents/src/agents/narrator.py`, `evals/e2e.py`, `evals/reports/2026-10-22-e2e-red_card_flip-3.json`, `packages/mcp_match/src/mcp_match/server.py` (`get_beat_bundle`) and `packages/intel/src/intel/since.py`.

The verification loop works: 29 of 30 bad claims caught and no good claims blocked. But the claims in the end-to-end run read like a stat sheet or a database, not commentary. The judges will see these lines on screen, and they are the product's voice. Examples from the run:

- "Ashford Vale's away-10 fires a 0.30 xG shot at 9′ that is blocked and not on target after a 4 second sequence."
- "Away player gets a second_yellow and is sent off, Ashford are down to 10 at 63′."
- "Ashford Vale have taken a bigger share of possession since ev-001484, rising to 54% in that spell." (blocked)
- "The move starts 7 m from where the ball was won, with 0 passes and 0 attackers ahead of the ball."
- "…shooting 3 seconds after the ball was won at 97:22."

**Causes:**
1. `evals/e2e.py` builds facts without the roster, so shooters are ids. The Narrator prompt then tells the model to use ids.
2. Facts reach the Narrator by machine name (`back_line_height(away, since ev-001484 to t5571.00)`), so the model copies internal tokens like `ev-001484`, `second_yellow` and "since the event", and invents span lengths ("the last 11 minutes").
3. The prompt says "use two to four facts" and "stay literal", so the model lists every fact, zeros included.
4. Clock facts render as `97:22`, not the broadcast label `90+7′`.

Before writing: restate the task back in eight lines or fewer and wait for my go.

## Parts

1. **Names, not ids.**
   - `evals/e2e.py`, `evals/run.py` and any other path that builds a registry pass the brief's roster, as `live.py` already does.
   - Narrator rule: refer to players by the name a fact gives. A player with no name fact is "the Ashford No. 10" style, never `away-10`.
2. **Fact labels for the Narrator.** `get_beat_bundle` adds a plain-English `label` to each fact. The label is written by code from the fact's name and scope, in one module with named templates, never by a model. For example:

   | Fact | Label |
   | --- | --- |
   | back-line height, Ashford, before the red card | "Ashford back line, 15 minutes before the red card" |
   | the same, since the card | "Ashford back line since the red card" |
   | shot xG | "chance quality (xG)" |
   | card kind | "card: second yellow, sent off" |
   | a "since" span | "since the red card (63′)" or "since Rivermouth's goal (71′)" |

   - Anchors are named by their event (red card, goal, corner), never by id.
   - The Narrator sees `label` plus the display renderings, **not** the machine name.
   - Clock facts also get a broadcast rendering (`90+7′`, `45+2′`), and `sequence.start_clock` and `sequence.shot_clock` render as minutes, never `mm:ss`.
3. **Narrator prompt `narrator.v2.md`.** Keep v1 for the record.
   - **Pick the one or two facts that tell the story,** not all of them. In `fan` mode, at most two numbers per claim, and never a zero count ("0 passes", "0 attackers"). Omit the fact instead.
   - **Football verbs that restate an event are fine:** "scores", "levels", "breaks", "wins it back", "hits the post", "is sent off". Adjectives and causes still need a fact.
   - `fan`: 1 or 2 claims per beat. `analyst`: up to 3 claims and may use xG and positional numbers.
   - Remove the "refer to a player by id" rule.
4. **Code catches what slips through.** Add `error` rules to `voice/banned.yaml` for narration:
   - player ids `\b(home|away)-\d{2}\b`;
   - event ids `\bev-\d{6}\b`;
   - snake_case tokens `\b[a-z]+_[a-z_]+\b`;
   - `\bsince the event\b`;
   - `\bfreeze frame\b`;
   - `\bzone (attackers|defenders)\b`;
   - `\b\d{2,3}:\d{2}\b` (clock time).

   Add `warn` rules for a zero count (`\b0 (passes|attackers|defenders|shots)\b`) and for a claim with more than three numbers. These reach the Checker through the existing voice-lint step.
5. **Readability checks in the end-to-end report.** `evals/e2e.py` reports, per run:
   - claims with any voice-lint violation;
   - average numbers per claim;
   - any id or snake_case token in published text (must be 0);
   - claims per beat;
   - beat latency p50 and p90.

   **Target:** 0 ids or machine tokens, at most 2.0 numbers per fan claim on average, and beat p90 at most 8 s.
6. **Re-run** `make evals` (judge `gpt-5-mini`) to confirm nothing regressed: still at least 28 of 30 caught and at most 1 good claim blocked. Re-run the end-to-end on `red_card_flip` seed 3 in `fan` and `analyst` modes.
