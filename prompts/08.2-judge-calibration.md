The judge is inconsistent in the two directions that matter most for the demo.

**Too strict on football verbs.** It amended or blocked:
- "smashes a 0.14 xG shot in";
- "lashes a shot off the post";
- "Rivermouth press";
- "sit higher".

`VOICE.md` allows verbs that describe the action. A Checker that objects to "smashes" looks pedantic on screen.

**Too lenient on motive and intent.** It verified:
- "…as they push for a response";
- "…as they try to see this through";
- "…as they chase an equaliser";
- "Ashford are pinned back";
- "Ashford are left short upfront".

These state motives or team states that no fact supports. Catching that is the Checker's core job.

**Two bundle gaps:**
- The card fact has no player name ("Ashford's player is shown a second yellow").
- Follow-up bundles include trivial changes, so the analyst run narrated "compactness improving by 1 square metre".

Before writing: restate the task back in six lines or fewer and wait for my go.

## Parts

1. **`judge.v2.md`** (keep v1 for the record). The rubric, stated plainly:
   - **Allowed without a fact:** verbs that describe the action itself (fires, smashes, lashes, curls, nods, rattles the post, scores, levels, breaks, wins it back), provided the event and its outcome match the facts.
   - **Needs a fact:**
     - descriptions of a team's state or control ("on top", "pinned back", "in control", "dominant", "tired", "rattled");
     - motives and intent ("as they chase…", "to see it through", "push for a response", "to manage the game");
     - causes ("so…", "because…", "which lets…");
     - comparisons and superlatives;
     - anything about a period the facts don't cover.

   Keep the existing problem categories and add `motive` for intent and motive clauses. Point `models.yaml` at v2.
2. **Evals:** add 8 bad claims built on real beats, each a correct statement plus a motive or team-state clause: "as they chase an equaliser", "pinned back", "to see the game out", "piling on the pressure" and similar, all with expected `rephrased` or `blocked`. Add 4 good claims that use vivid action verbs on supported events, expected `verified`. The set becomes 38 bad and 14 good. Update the pick rule proportionally: at least 35 of 38 caught, at most 1 of 14 good blocked, p90 at most 4 s.
3. **Card facts** carry the player's name from the roster. Labels and the bundle show it ("Dunmore, second yellow, sent off").
4. **Trim follow-up bundles.** In `get_beat_bundle`, for `momentum_swing` beats, include a side's change facts (and their before and since pair) only when that change meets the beat's own threshold: back line at least 4 m, compactness at least 10%, possession or territory at least 10 points. Always include the facts that triggered the beat.
5. **Re-run, in order:**
   1. `make bakeoff` with all three candidates on v2. If `judge-grok` returns 429 again, record it and rerun it alone once after 10 minutes.
   2. The end-to-end run on `red_card_flip` seed 3 in `fan` and `analyst` modes with the winning judge.

   Append the result to ADR 0002's Decision log (Oct 22, evening) and update `evals/reports/bakeoff.md`.
